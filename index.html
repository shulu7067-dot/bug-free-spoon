Our story

Born in Soweto.
Built for All of SA.

Khwezi Drive was founded in 2006 in Soweto by Thabo Nkosi, a SADTI-registered driving instructor who believed that quality driving education should be available to every South African — not just those who could afford private tutors in the northern suburbs.

What started as one instructor, one car, and a handwritten lesson schedule has grown into an academy with 18 certified instructors, branches in 8 provinces, and a pass rate that consistently doubles the national average.

Our name means morning star in Zulu — the first light before the road opens up. That's what we aim to be for every learner who walks through our doors.

Common Questions

Do I need my learner's licence before booking a practical lesson?

Yes — you need a valid learner's licence to legally drive on South African public roads. We offer a K53 learner's package to help you pass the theory test first, and then we move straight into practical lessons.

How many lessons will I need to pass my driver's test?

The national average is 20–30 hours of instruction. Our structured programme typically achieves test-readiness in 10–15 hours, depending on prior experience and how frequently you book. We give you an honest estimate at your first session.

Can I use your car for my DLTC test?

Yes. We offer a test-day vehicle hire (R450) which includes a 30-minute warm-up lesson in the test area beforehand. Most students find this makes a significant difference to their confidence on the day.

What is the pass guarantee?

If you complete our Standard or Fast-Track package and don't pass your DLTC test on the first attempt, we provide two additional 1-hour preparation lessons free of charge before your retest. No expiry on the guarantee.

Do you offer lessons in languages other than English?

Yes — several of our instructors can teach in Zulu, Sotho, Xhosa, and Afrikaans. Let us know your language preference when booking and we'll match you accordingly.

How do I pay for lessons?

We accept EFT, card (in-branch), and SnapScan. Cash is accepted at all branches. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your slot. Packages are paid in full upfront and include all sessions listed.
